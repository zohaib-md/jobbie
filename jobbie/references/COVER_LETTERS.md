# Cover letters

Use `cover-letter draft --stdin` to produce a truthful draft from verified facts.

## Workflow

1. Confirm company and role from the direct posting.
2. Pass only résumé-backed `highlights`.
3. Show the draft to the candidate for edits.
4. Paste the approved text into the application form manually or via browser automation.

## Do not

- Invent metrics, team sizes, or business outcomes.
- Claim interest in mission statements the candidate has not endorsed.
- Auto-submit a cover letter in `review-each` mode without explicit approval.
