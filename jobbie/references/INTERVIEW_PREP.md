# Interview prep

Use `prep generate --stdin` after an interview is scheduled.

## Output

The command returns:

- role themes inferred from the posting;
- behavioral prompts grounded in verified experience;
- technical prompts based on matched ATS keywords;
- talking points from the profile and résumé.

## Candidate review

- Replace generic prompts with STAR stories from verified résumé bullets.
- Remove any question that would require claiming unverified skills.
- Record interview outcomes with `ledger outcome --stdin`.
